## What the caller may say

- `bound` is local state. `binding_rejected` means repeated authenticated issuer rejection, **not deletion**. Network/TLS failure is not a rejection. A running connector is not evidence the old device key is accepted. Preserve a previously bound Off preference.
- `pairing_recovery_required` means the protected operation may have committed upstream. Resume its exact request/result. A lost one-shot claim reply before journal handoff may instead need `fresh_claim_required`. Don't report rollback. Published `binding_disposition: existing|new` is issuer evidence, not an ID comparison.
- `active_pairing_recent` and `paired_installation_loopback_required` do not authorize reset. Only a proved `installation_unverified` with `recovery: reset_as_new` and an allowed reason permits Web preview: `predecessor_unrecoverable` has no hint; `other_account` has only the validated masked hint. Offer the prior account first. Detaching does not notify it or delete the old hosted Avibe.
- `resetting` closes enrollment even after the config looks blank. `reset_repair_required` means the marker cannot safely advance. `operation_in_progress`, `local_binding_changed`, `confirmation_expired`, `reset_not_eligible` and `local_operator_required` are not completion. Re-read the local state instead of bypassing it. A rejected committed rename may end locally as `reconciliation_interrupted`; the hosted name may already have changed.
- Direct socket loopback, Host, Origin/CSRF and invocation checks are enforced; they are **not proof of physical presence**. Same-origin script and unmarked same-OS-user code are inside the documented trust boundary. Keep the installation UUID, device keys, callback proof, PKCE material and reset confirmation out of command output, diagnostics and telemetry. No new recovery-ID readout.

## Cutover and verification

Reset seals intent before cleanup and keeps the fence on error. Binding publication retires remote registry/session and push authority before config save. A remote sender or address-cleaning response already admitted must drain before publication; a dead process releases its lease. Late provider results cannot disable a newly registered endpoint reusing the same URL. Local subscriptions and workspace survive. Existing remote streams lose authority on revalidation; material already handed off cannot be recalled. Pending account sign-outs are not re-signed with new credentials. Live Health owner decisions must match the observed generation and enrollment: same identity keeps its days, a confirmed different identity purges stale data; explicit reset purges even offline.

From the worktree: `.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q --disable-warnings @docs/plans/av13-backend-tests.txt`. **177 isolated checks passed** in the combined selection. Includes synthetic hosted replies, authorized and rejected browser flows, real temporary SQLite/Health stores, interrupted stages, stale/replaced files, and separate processes for reset/publication vs active cleanup (normal release and process exit). HTTP/SSE and the WebSocket authorization-loss waiter are exercised; push delivery hands off to a fake provider, not a real phone. No real account, desktop UI, clipboard, keychain, service restart or production tunnel was used.
