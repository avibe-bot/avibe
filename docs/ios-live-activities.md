# Follow a Turn on the iPhone Lock Screen

In **Settings > Notifications**, turn on **Show progress on the Lock Screen**. The setting belongs to this device and starts off. When you leave Avibe while the conversation you are viewing is still working, its progress appears on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. The conversation page has no Lock Screen control.

The card shows the Agent name, the conversation title, the state and elapsed time. Names and titles are captured on the phone when tracking starts; renaming the conversation does not change an existing card. Message text, tool names and files are not included. The relay and Apple receive only the state and timestamps.

When the Turn needs an answer or approval, the card changes to **Needs you** and can light the screen. Tap it to return to the conversation and respond there. Completed, failed and stopped Turns remain on the Lock Screen for one hour. A running activity ends after eight hours. If the instance has not confirmed its state for forty minutes, the card indicates that it may be out of date.

Dismissing a card stops tracking that Turn on this device; leaving the App again does not recreate it. Turn the device setting off to end its activities. Signing out or switching accounts or instances also ends them immediately.

If Live Activities are disabled in system Settings, the switch is disabled and offers **Settings**. Native notifications must also be enabled so the phone has a push registration. The device switch appears only in an App version that supports automatic Live Activities.
